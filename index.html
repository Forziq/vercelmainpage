Solana Journal

A private web app for reviewing trades. It reads a wallet's trades from the Solana blockchain, rebuilds them into positions and works out the statistics. Then it asks what a trader usually skips: did you follow your plan, and what would you do again?

The demo runs on invented data. Turn on Explain and every screen tells you how it works.
