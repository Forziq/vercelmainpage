How to apply it

  1. Keep the backing on until you're ready.
  2. Cut the image while it's still on the backing.
  3. Lower it onto the iced cake from the middle outwards, then smooth gently.

Delivery and returns

Delivery

Delivery is free anywhere in Ireland. We print and post your order within 1 working day, then it takes up to 2 days in the post. It's packed in a grip-seal bag inside a board-backed A4 envelope.

Returns

For food hygiene reasons, we can't accept returns for ordering mistakes, a change of mind or late arrival. If your image is damaged or printed incorrectly, we can replace it once it's been sent back to us.
